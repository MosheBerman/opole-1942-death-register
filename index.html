A searchable transcription of the 1942 death register of the Jewish community of Opole (Opole Lubelskie), Poland. Most of those recorded here were Jews deported from Vienna in 1941. Each entry gives the name, age, recorded birthplace, date and time of death, and witnesses exactly as the register records them, with the Hebrew date of death and the yahrtzeit, and what DÖW, Yad Vashem, and the US Holocaust Memorial Museum record as the person's fate. Original scans: Szukaj w Archiwach, unit 39731697.

Transcribed from the register scans published by the Archiwum Państwowe w Lublinie via Szukaj w Archiwach. Birthplaces, ages, and causes are as recorded in 1942 and may differ from other sources; the register itself is the authority for what it says. Fate entries quote each database's own wording and link to its record. A probable match is a record whose name matches but whose stated birth year falls within two years of the age implied by the register; it is marked, never merged silently. Birthplaces are shown as recorded; the filter groups spelling and language variants of the same place under a standardized name.
