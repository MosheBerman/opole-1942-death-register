A searchable transcription of the Jewish death register kept at Opole Lubelskie in 1942. Most of the dead were Jews deported from Vienna in February and March 1941, and Jews brought from Slovakia in 1942. Each entry gives the name, age, recorded birthplace, date and time of death, witnesses, and the yahrtzeit (Hebrew date of death), and what DÖW, Yad Vashem, and the US Holocaust Memorial Museum record as the person's fate.

Month × cause of death (bubbles)

One bubble per gender in each month and cause cell. Bubble size = deaths; colour = gender; the number inside a bubble is the median age of that group. The eight most common causes are shown, grouped as elsewhere on this page; the rest fall under Other.

Witness pairs

The two witnesses (declarants) served in stretches; a new pair marks a changeover. Pairs are ordered by first appearance; witness names are standardized, spelling variants grouped.

Transcribed from the register scans published by the Archiwum Państwowe w Lublinie via Szukaj w Archiwach. Birthplaces, ages, and causes are as recorded in 1942 and may differ from other sources; the register itself is the authority for what it says. Fate entries quote each database's own wording and link to its record. A probable match is a record whose name matches but whose stated birth year falls within two years of the age implied by the register; it is marked, never merged silently. Birthplaces are shown as recorded; the filter groups spelling and language variants of the same place under a standardized name. Witness names are standardized the same way.
